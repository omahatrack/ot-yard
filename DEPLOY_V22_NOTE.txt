OTE Equipment Hub v22

Service-document enhancement:
- Service records now have an explicit Service Date field.
- PDFs and service images can be attached while recording service.
- Existing service records can receive documents later.
- Each attachment has a human-readable document name.
- Optional document notes support invoice/work-order/vendor references.
- Upload date is recorded automatically.
- Documents display inside Service History and Equipment Service History.
- Authorized Admin/Mechanic users can open or remove attachments.
- Attachments inherit the service record's service date rather than maintaining a conflicting second service date.
- Files are stored in the application database and protected by the same location permissions as the equipment.
- Allowed: PDF, JPG, PNG, WEBP; maximum 15 MB per file.

All v21 functionality retained.
